Quan (Lane Detection)
Team Leader • Lane Boundary Subsystem
Baseline 1 Canny-Hough
Dataset: CULane (Trapezoidal ROI)
J. Canny (IEEE TPAMI 1986)
Duda & Hart (CACM 1972) • Handcrafted Edge & Line Detection
🏛️ IEEE TPAMI 1986 ↗
⚡ Speed-First / Classical CPU Baseline
🎯 Accuracy (F1 CULane): < 40.0% (Poor)
⏱️ Speed / Latency: > 100 FPS (~10 ms)
Critical Failure
Hand-crafted gradient thresholds fail catastrophically under tree shadows, road cracks, night headlight glare, and worn-out pavement markings.
➔ Rationale: Establishes the lower bound of non-learning methods.
Baseline 2 SCNN (Spatial CNN)
Dataset: CULane (133k frames, 9 scenarios)
X. Pan et al. (IEEE/CVF CVPR 2018)
Original Paper publishing CULane Dataset (CUHK / SenseTime)
📄 arXiv:1712.06080 ↗
🎯 Accuracy-First / Seq DL CULane Official
🎯 Accuracy (F1 CULane): 71.60% (High)
⏱️ Speed / Latency: ~7.5 FPS (133 ms)
Compute Bottleneck
Slice-by-slice spatial convolution (Top➔Down, Left➔Right) imposes strict sequential dependencies, blocking GPU parallel execution and causing unacceptable latency (> 100 ms).
➔ Rationale: Proves the necessity of anchor-based classification (UFLDv2: > 120 FPS).
Selected Baseline UFLDv2 ResNet-18
Dataset: CULane Benchmark
New Paper
Proposed ADAS Architecture (Capstone Research 2026)
📄 Reference: IEEE TPAMI 2022 ↗
🚀 Selected Target Expected Objective
🎯 Target Accuracy (F1): ≥ 76.50% (Expected)
⏱️ Target Speed / Latency: > 120 FPS (~7.5 ms)
Selection Justification
Adopts row-based anchor classification over grid scan lines, bypassing the sequential bottleneck of SCNN while achieving high noise resistance over Canny. Augmented with B-Spline interpolation to eliminate frame jitter.
➔ Resolved: Delivers both real-time throughput (> 120 FPS) and high accuracy (F1 ≥ 76.5%).
Triet (Detection & Tracking)
Member • Object Detection & Multi-Object Tracking
Baseline 1 YOLOv8s
Dataset: MS COCO Pretrain & BDD100K Val
G. Jocher et al. (Ultralytics 2023)
State-of-the-Art Real-Time Vision Framework
🐙 Ultralytics YOLOv8 ↗
🎯 High Capacity / One-to-Many COCO / BDD100K
🎯 Accuracy (mAP₅₀): ~62.5% (High Cap)
⚠️ NMS Overhead (Tail p95): +2.5 ~ 4.0 ms (Jitter)
Latency Jitter
Standard One-to-Many label assignment mandates post-processing Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS). Bounding box count surges in crowded traffic, inducing severe tail latency (p95) instability.
➔ Rationale: Benchmarks NMS overhead eliminated by YOLOv10s Dual Heads.
Baseline 2 YOLOv10n
Dataset: BDD100K (10 ADAS classes, 2.27M)
A. Wang et al. (arXiv 2024)
Tsinghua University • NMS-free Real-Time Object Detection
📄 arXiv:2405.14458 ↗
⚡ NMS-Free / Lower-Bound 2.27M Params
🎯 Accuracy (mAP₅₀): 56.41% (Constrained)
⚠️ NMS Overhead (Tail p95): 0.0 ms (NMS-Free 4.5ms)
Capacity Limit
Severe parameter pruning restricts high-level feature abstraction, degrading detection accuracy on small objects, occluded vehicles, and distant pedestrians (VRUs).
➔ Rationale: Sets the compute-capacity sweet spot justifying YOLOv10s (8.0M params).
Selected Baseline YOLOv10s NMS-free
Dataset: BDD100K (10 ADAS Classes)
New Paper
Proposed ADAS Architecture (Capstone Research 2026)
📄 Reference: arXiv 2024 ↗
🚀 Selected Target Expected Objective
🎯 Target Accuracy (mAP₅₀): ≥ 58 ~ 60% (Expected)
⚠️ NMS Overhead (Tail p95): 0.0 ms (NMS-Free 140 FPS)
Selection Justification
Dual Label Assignment trains with One-to-Many supervisory signal but infers strictly via One-to-One head, eliminating post-processing NMS entirely while preserving 8.0M parameter capacity (vs underpowered 2.27M Nano).
➔ Resolved: Stabilizes tail latency (Full Track 72.2 FPS, p95 = 15.38 ms) without compromising VRU detection.
Khang (Depth & Collision)
Member • Monocular Depth & FCW Subsystem
Baseline 1 StereoSGBM
Dataset: KITTI Stereo 2015 / Raw
H. Hirschmuller (IEEE TPAMI 2008)
Stereo Processing by Semi-Global Matching and Mutual Info
🏛️ IEEE TPAMI 2008 ↗
📷 Hardware Rig / Geometric KITTI Stereo
📷 Sensor Architecture: Dual Stereo Rig
📏 Distance Metric (Z): Physical Meters (m)
Hardware Rigidity
Mandates strict dual-camera hardware synchronization and rigid baseline calibration. Mechanical vibration or thermal expansion rapidly disrupts stereo alignment, leading to NaN disparity.
➔ Rationale: Demonstrates why monocular camera setups are preferred for commercial vehicle ADAS.
Baseline 2 Depth Anything V1
Dataset: KITTI Eigen Split (Zero-shot)
L. Yang et al. (IEEE/CVF CVPR 2024)
Foundation Model for Relative Depth Estimation (TikTok / HKU)
📄 arXiv:2401.10891 ↗
👁️ Monocular / Relative DL Foundation Model
📷 Sensor Architecture: Single Monocular Cam
📏 Distance Metric (Z): Relative [0, 1] (No m)
Missing Metric Scale
Relative depth values lack absolute physical distance (meters). Impossible to compute real-world vehicle approach velocity (v_z = dZ/dt) and Time-to-Collision (TTC = Z / v_z) for ISO 15623 compliance.
➔ Rationale: Proves the critical need to upgrade to Monocular Metric Depth (Depth Anything V2).
Selected Baseline Depth Anything V2 ViT-S
Dataset: KITTI Raw & CARLA Synthetic
New Paper
Proposed ADAS Architecture (Capstone Research 2026)
📄 Reference: arXiv 2024 ↗
🚀 Selected Target Expected Objective
📷 Sensor Architecture: Single Monocular Cam
📏 Metric Distance (Z): Metric Meters (AbsRel < 0.12)
Selection Justification
Combines the mechanical simplicity and affordability of a single monocular camera with trained physical metric depth estimation, overcoming the unitless ambiguity of V1 while avoiding rigid stereo camera alignment.
➔ Resolved: Enables direct real-world TTC computation ($TTC = Z / v_z$) adhering to ISO 15623 warning thresholds.
